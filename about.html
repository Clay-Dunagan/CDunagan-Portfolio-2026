Process

A practical path from uncertainty to delivery

The exact process changes with each project, but the objective stays consistent: understand what matters, make informed decisions, and keep the work moving toward a usable result. I connect research, strategy, design, development, and delivery instead of treating them as disconnected stages.

  1. 01

    Clarify the problem and priorities

    I begin by understanding the people involved, the business goals, the available evidence, the technical realities, and the constraints that will shape the work.

  2. 02

    Turn findings into structure

    I translate what I learn into positioning, requirements, content hierarchy, information architecture, user flows, and a clearer plan for what needs to be created.

  3. 03

    Design, test, and refine

    I develop wireframes, prototypes, and interfaces with responsive behavior, accessibility, and real use in mind. Feedback and testing help reveal what should be clarified, simplified, or reconsidered.

  4. 04

    Carry decisions through delivery

    I carry the work as far into implementation as the project requires, supporting front-end development, quality assurance, launch, handoff, and continued improvement so the finished experience stays connected to its original goals.

Where I Do My Best Work

I do my best work when the path is not obvious.

I enjoy projects that begin with competing needs, incomplete information, or an experience that no longer works as well as it should. I bring structure to that uncertainty, help people make practical decisions, and stay involved until the work becomes useful, credible, and ready to move forward.

Whether I am working within a team or directly with a client, I value clear communication, thoughtful collaboration, and solutions that serve both the people using them and the business responsible for them.
