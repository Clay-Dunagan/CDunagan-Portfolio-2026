Inside the Case Studies

The decisions behind the work

Each case study explains what the project required, what I was responsible for, and how research, business context, design decisions, and delivery shaped the result. The depth varies because the projects reached different stages and involved different constraints.

  1. 01

    Problem and Context

    Why the project existed, who it needed to serve, what was not working, and which constraints shaped the work.

  2. 02

    Research and Direction

    The evidence, stakeholder input, user research, audits, and business priorities that helped establish a clearer direction.

  3. 03

    Decisions and Execution

    How findings were translated into positioning, information architecture, user flows, interfaces, content, and front-end implementation where relevant.

  4. 04

    Validation and Outcome

    What was tested, launched, improved, or learned, with a clear distinction between completed client work and earlier-stage product concepts.
